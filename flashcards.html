How the boxes work

Every term starts in the New box. Each time you rate a card, it moves:

  • Again sends it back to New — you'll see it again tomorrow.
  • Good promotes it one box: New → Learning → Known. Learning cards return in 3 days, Known cards in a week.
  • Easy jumps it straight to Known.

This is the classic Leitner system: cards you struggle with come back often, cards you know fade into the background, so your revision time lands where it's needed. Progress is stored only in this browser (sc-cards). Clearing your browser data resets it.
