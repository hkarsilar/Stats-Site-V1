The Research Toolkit

Writing: Reporting Your Research

A result nobody can read is a result nobody will use. The last stretch of any project is writing it up, and it trips up more students than the statistics did. These eight lessons make the write-up mechanical in the best way, so the words come easily once you know which sentence goes where.

The frame is IMRaD: why you looked, how you looked, what you found, what it means, each with its own room. Inside that frame the course gets specific about the parts people dread, APA statistics reporting down to the italics and the missing leading zero, figures that don't mislead, a clean results section that states findings without over-claiming, and the honest craft of writing up a null result. It ends with the discussion, the abstract and title, and a final checklist to catch the cheap mistakes before a grader does.

Most useful right when you have output in hand and a blank document open. No prerequisites beyond a study to report; it closes out the Methods → Data → Writing arc of the Toolkit.
