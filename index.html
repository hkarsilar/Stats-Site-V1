Accessibility

StatsCapybara aims to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Every page is keyboard-operable (there's a skip link, visible focus rings, and a focus-trapped search), text meets 4.5:1 contrast in both light and dark themes, form controls and charts are labeled for screen readers, live readouts announce as you move a slider, and the site honors your system's reduce motion setting.

The interactive visualizations are canvas graphics, so they lean on sight by nature, but each pairs its picture with text readouts, and the controls that drive it (sliders, buttons, and arrow-key nudges on the drag-based ones) work without a mouse. If something is hard to use with a keyboard or screen reader, please tell me. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a finished checkbox.
