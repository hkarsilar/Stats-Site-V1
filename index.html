About the instructor

Hi, I'm Dr. Hakan Karsilar — a scientist and lecturer at University College Groningen, University of Groningen. I built this site because I believe statistics doesn't have to be intimidating. With the right visualizations and a bit of hands-on practice, anyone can build real intuition for how data works.

Every section is designed from the ground up — no hand-waving, no "just trust the formula." You'll see each concept in action, work through real examples, and test yourself along the way. If you're curious about the research behind the teaching, my publications are on Google Scholar.
